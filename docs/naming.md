# Naming shortlist and comparison

The product name is not selected. The public repository remains `brand-agent-workspace`. The brand one-pager uses **AgentsByBrand** as the recommendation, not as an approved final name.

The owner requested names using **Agent** or **Agentic**, with direct meaning. Scores below are editorial judgments out of five, not survey results.

| Rank | Name | Immediate clarity | Fit for this product | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **AgentsByBrand** | 5 | 5 | Most explicit about multiple agents organized by brand; longer than Roma. |
| 2 | **Agent BrandDesk** | 4 | 5 | Suggests a useful home for brand work; a little more cumbersome to say. |
| 3 | **Agent ContextDesk** | 4 | 4 | Emphasizes the shared context problem; needs a descriptor to communicate client/brand scope. |
| 4 | **Agentic Brandroom** | 3 | 4 | Sounds like a shared working environment; “agentic” needs explanation for nontechnical buyers. |
| 5 | **Agentic Roster** | 3 | 3 | Strong for organizing agents and responsibilities; sounds more like a directory than a task system. |

Recommendation: **AgentsByBrand** with the descriptor **The open-source agent workspace for every brand**. Prefer “agent” over “agentic” for a broad audience because it names the things being managed.

## Against the reference products

| Name | What the name communicates | What needs explaining | Our differentiation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paperclip | A familiar office object; memorable and broad. | Its agent-orchestration function. | Explicit brand, contact, profile, and account context. Paperclip is an optional backend here, not a copied codebase. |
| Retinue | A group of attendants; relevant to a team of agents. | The unfamiliar word and the task workflow. | Direct language aimed at client-service operators. |
| Roma | Short, easy to say, broadly brandable. | What it does; the tagline carries the category. | A name that explains the organizing principle immediately. |
| AgentsByBrand | Agents organized by brand. | That it also preserves contacts, profiles, account references, and history. | Strongest fit for the specific workflow being built. |

This compares positioning and naming, not production feature parity. Paperclip's documented scope is substantially broader than this pilot.

## Preliminary collision check

Web searches on November 11, 2026 surfaced existing uses of [AgentRoster](https://www.agentroster.ai/), [AgentBrief](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=agentbrief.agentbrief), and [AgenticDesk](https://www.agenticdesk.co.uk/), so these were excluded. Exact searches for the shortlisted phrases did not establish an obvious exact competing product. Search results are not domain availability or trademark clearance; neither was established.

Reference products: [Paperclip](https://github.com/paperclipai/paperclip), [Retinue](https://github.com/jklthinking/retinue), [Roma](https://roma.app/).
